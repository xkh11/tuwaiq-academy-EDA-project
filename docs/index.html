Bologna's Airbnb market rewards the middle

Most listings sit in two central neighbourhoods, a few professional hosts run over a quarter of them, and mid-priced places attract the most guests.

What I learned

  • Cleaning comes first: 518 missing prices and 44 extreme values had to go before any average made sense.
  • For skewed prices, the median describes a typical night better than the mean.
  • A model must be compared with a baseline to be honest.

Limitations

  • One snapshot (September 2026), so no seasons.
  • Listed prices, not what guests paid.
  • Reviews only approximate demand.
  • The price model explains only 16% of price differences.

Future work

  • Compare seasons and other Italian cities.
  • Use the detailed file: review scores, amenities, bedrooms.
  • Improve the price model with those features.
